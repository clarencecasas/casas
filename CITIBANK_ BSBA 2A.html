Company Background

Citibank was founded on June 16, 1812, as the City Bank of New York. Over the years, it has grown through a series of mergers and acquisitions to become one of the "Big Four" banks in the United States and a globally recognized financial institution. The bank's history spans more than two centuries, evolving from a modest financial institution to a global banking powerhouse.

The bank was renamed First National City Bank of New York in 1962, then shortened to First National City Bank in 1968, and finally became Citibank in 1976. Citibank is now the consumer division of financial services multinational Citigroup.

Corporate Profile

Citibank is the consumer division of financial services multinational Citigroup. The bank has approximately 2,649 branches in 19 countries, including 723 branches in the United States and 1,494 branches in Mexico operated by its subsidiary Banamex.

The bank is a leader in multiple sectors, including:

Consumer Banking

The company employs approximately 200,000 staff worldwide and holds over $1.7 trillion in assets, making it one of the largest banking institutions globally.

Vision, Mission, Goals and Objectives, and Core Values

Mission Statement

Citibank's mission is to serve as a trusted partner to its clients by responsibly providing financial services that enable growth and economic progress. This encompasses safeguarding assets, lending money, making payments, and accessing capital markets on behalf of clients. With over 200 years of experience, Citibank connects millions across hundreds of countries and cities, aiming to create the best outcomes for clients and customers through simple, creative, and responsible financial solutions.

Vision Statement

Citibank envisions being the preeminent banking partner for institutions with cross-border needs, a global leader in wealth management, and a valued personal bank in its home market of the United States. This vision underscores its commitment to excellence in consulting, client advocacy, and the delivery of experienced services that exceed client expectations.

Goals and Objectives

  • Digital Transformation: Accelerating the modernization of banking services to meet the demands of the digital age.
  • Client-Centric Approach: Providing personalized financial strategies to foster long-term client relationships.
  • Sustainable Development: Committing to environmental and social responsibility, with significant investments in sustainable finance.
  • Risk Management Optimization: Enhancing risk management frameworks to ensure prudent financial practices.
  • Inclusive Financial Services: Expanding access to financial services to underserved communities worldwide.

Core Values

  • Common Purpose: Operating as one team with the unified goal of serving clients and stakeholders.
  • Responsible Finance: Conducting business with transparency, prudence, and dependability.
  • Ingenuity: Enhancing clients' lives through innovative solutions that leverage the bank's global network and expertise.
  • Leadership: Fostering a culture where talented individuals thrive in a diverse meritocracy that demands excellence and initiative.

These values are integral to Citibank's commitment to ethical practices, client satisfaction, and societal impact.

Citigroup continued its strategy to exit consumer banking operations in several markets, including the Philippines, focusing instead on institutional and corporate banking services.

Structural Simplification

The reorganization eliminated previous management layers, including the Personal Banking & Wealth Management and Institutional Clients Group divisions, as well as regional layers in Asia Pacific, Europe, Middle East and Africa, and Latin America. This flatter structure aims to accelerate decision-making and enhance client focus.

International Operations

Leadership of geographies outside North America is consolidated under Ernesto Torres Cantú, Head of International. This role focuses on local-market client coverage, delivery, and legal entity management, promoting greater connectivity across Citi's global operations.

Client Organization

A newly established Client organization, led by Chief Client Officer David Livingstone, is responsible for strengthening client engagement and experience across all business lines and regions.

This organizational structure reflects Citibank's strategic focus on simplifying operations, enhancing accountability, and delivering superior client service.

Regulatory Stress Testing

Citigroup successfully passed the Federal Reserve's 2024 Comprehensive Capital Analysis and Review (CCAR), leading to a reduction in its capital ratio requirement from 12.3% to 12.1%. This outcome reflects the bank's improved risk profile and capital planning.

Capital Return to Shareholders

In Q1 2025, Citigroup returned approximately $2.8 billion to common shareholders through dividends and share repurchases. This continued commitment to shareholder returns underscores the bank's confidence in its capital position.

Citigroup's capital structure and adequacy as of April 2025 demonstrate a strong financial foundation, strategic debt management, and a commitment to delivering value to shareholders while maintaining regulatory compliance.

Corporate Social Responsibility

As of 2025, Citigroup Inc. continues to demonstrate its commitment to corporate social responsibility (CSR) through a variety of initiatives that address economic inclusion, environmental sustainability, community development, and access to essential services.

🌍 2025 Global Innovation Challenge – Youth Employability

The Citi Foundation has launched the 2025 Global Innovation Challenge, providing $500,000 grants to 50 community organizations worldwide. This initiative aims to accelerate youth employability by supporting programs focused on upskilling, entrepreneurship, and financial education for low-income youth. Building on the success of the Pathways to Progress initiative, which has invested over $300 million to support more than one million young people globally, this challenge underscores Citi's commitment to empowering the next generation.

🏘️ Affordable Housing and Homelessness Solutions

In 2024, the Citi Foundation's Global Innovation Challenge addressed homelessness by awarding $25 million in grants to 50 nonprofit organizations. These funds support innovative solutions to housing insecurity, including the development of affordable housing projects with green building certifications. Citi has also financed and facilitated over $30 billion toward affordable housing globally between 2020 and 2022, demonstrating a sustained commitment to housing stability.

🌱 Sustainable Progress Strategy

Citi's 2025 Sustainable Progress Strategy includes a $250 billion Environmental Finance Goal over five years. This strategy focuses on financing projects in renewable energy, clean technology, sustainable transportation, and more. By supporting clients in transitioning to low-carbon business models, Citi aims to drive environmental sustainability and economic growth.

🏥 CITI Cares – Community Health Initiatives

Through the CITI Cares program, Citi engages in various community health initiatives, such as establishing malnutrition-free villages for women and children and organizing free eye checkup camps for the elderly. These efforts reflect Citi's dedication to improving health outcomes and supporting vulnerable populations in the communities it serves.

💼 Social Finance Expansion in Asia

Citi anticipates a more than 10% increase in social finance funding in Asia for 2025. This growth encompasses various financing types, including social trade loans, co-financing partnerships with development agencies, and support for social enterprises. By expanding social finance, Citi aims to address pressing social issues and promote inclusive economic development across the region.

These initiatives exemplify Citi's multifaceted approach to corporate social responsibility, integrating economic empowerment, environmental stewardship, and community well-being into its global operations.
